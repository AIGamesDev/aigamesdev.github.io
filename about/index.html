THE MAN BEHIND IT ALL

I really got into coding back in 2021 during the pandemic, mostly because isolation left me with a lot of free time. I originally made this account in 2020, though I had an older one going back to 2017. My first projects weren’t great, but over time I kept improving, and now I feel like I’m making my best work yet. I became active on the Scratch forums in 2021, starting out by working in shops. Eventually, I shifted into bigger ideas and launched a collaboration called AspectOS, mainly to experience what it was like building something as a team. It ended up being my most successful project so far—even though I haven’t released a polished version yet. Outside of projects, I enjoy contributing to the community by helping others and engaging in discussions. I’ve made around 7,000 forum posts and, last I checked, ranked in the top 50 for most posts overall. In the Announcements forum, I even reached #1 with about 250–350 points. Since that forum closed to the public, I guess that record is staying put. In my free time, I like listening to music, playing video games, and finding creative ways around challenges. I’d say I’m good at problem-solving (and procrastinating—maybe a bit too good at that), but I always finish what I start. I’ve gone on hiatus a few times, which has delayed projects here and there, but I don’t plan on leaving Scratch anytime soon. My goal is to keep supporting the community and creating fun, meaningful projects for my nearly 500 followers. This website is dedicated to my Scratch journey. Here, you’ll find my projects (both old and new), unreleased experiments, and exclusive content that doesn’t make it onto Scratch. I’ll also post updates here, whether it’s news about delays, cancellations, or exciting progress on upcoming projects. I’ve put hours into building this site with my friend GPT, and while it’s still a work in progress, I’m proud of how it’s turning out. More features are coming soon, but for now—it feels just right.
