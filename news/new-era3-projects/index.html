NEW ERA 3 PROJECTS

Well I have finally decided on what to do with future Era 3 projects. I think I have taken a long enough break (even though this is like my third break in the past three months) to get back to work. Because of this, I have decided to make some new projects that will release this year as part of Era 3! Finally, new projects. First off, I want to say that I have delayed aOS again. This is because I need more time on it. Way more time on it. I have to redo the code for most of it because it just seems laggy. I wonder if this will release before GTA VI does. It probably won't considering that it's supposed to release this year. But I will try my best to get it out by summer of next year. I also want to say that I will be working on a few other projects as well. New simulation games will occur, in which you have taken position of real-world positions and are in charge of keeping everything in order. More on that later though.



First off I want to talk about Football Penalty Simulator. I have decided to make that the newest project that is coming to Era 3. I will try to make it ready for publishing by June. I am expecting for this to drop on June 25, 2025. Now I can't guarantee this considering the amount of breaks that I have had this year so far, so it could very well be delayed. You basically are a football (or soccer) manager and you have to try to win the league with your team, as well as keeping your position as manager. Start losing, and let's just say that the job is gone for you! Game over!



The next game I want to talk is Mighty Mayor. This is a game in which you have taken the position of mayor as a small town, and you have to make sure that everything stays in order. Satisfy the citizens of the town, keep the budget in check, and make sure that you don't get voted out of office. This is a game that I have been working on for a while now, and I am hoping to release it by August 25, 2025. This game will be a bit more complex than Football Penalty Simulator, but I think it will be worth it in the end. You will have to manage resources, deal with random events, and make tough decisions that will affect your town.



The next project I want to talk about is Meeby. Heavily inspired by Miis, Meebs are customizable avatars that may be used in future projects. These act as avatars for my projects, and was originally created when Aurora Inc was still a collaboration. Originally called Urmoji, the point of it was to act as an online character on Scratch that best matches your personality. I will try to get this project released in October in beta, with the full version possibly coming in 2026.



The last confirmed project that is planned is Patriotic President. It acts as a sequel to Mighty Mayor, in which you have won the presidential election and you have to run the country for the next four years. This will probably be a harder game as you have to manage an entire country, compared to a tiny town. Make sure that global relations are okay, avoid war, keep the stock market okay, and get the general approval of the people.



There are other projects that I have planned, such as a return to interviewing forumers, and maybe a sequel to Button Clickers with better everything. But I haven't though to go through with these ideas though. I kind of want to make high quality projects, and I feel like these Era 2 or Era 1 like projects won't cut it. More projects are expected to be announced soon. Maybe my next announcement will be in June for new projects and maybe a set date on the end of Era 3, and what I plan to do beyond that. Maybe retirement is an option, you never know...
